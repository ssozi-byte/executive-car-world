About the business

Two trades, one roof

Executive Car World Ltd keeps a parts counter and a working garage at the same address on Kiambu Road, so nothing has to leave the site to get done.

How the business works

Parts and labour sit in the same building. You can buy a battery, a set of headlights or a facelift kit across the counter and have it fitted in the bay without moving the car anywhere else.

The workshop takes the routine work — servicing, oil changes, tyre fitting, puncture repair, wheel alignment and balancing — and the heavier jobs as well: accident repair, panel work, full respray, suspension faults and gearbox rebuilds in a dedicated gearbox facility. Body repair and lighting are handled in house rather than sent out.

Parts also leave the country: the shop's own channels describe sending orders to more than fifty countries, alongside the customers who walk in off Kiambu Road.

The shop stands at Ridgeways on Kiambu Road, opposite Total Ridgeways, and is open six days a week. Everything the business does publicly — the repairs, the resprays, the facelifts and restorations — is posted from the shop's own accounts, which is the easiest way to see the work before you commit the car to it.
