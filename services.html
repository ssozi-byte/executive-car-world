The garage

Everything the bay can take

Eight kinds of work, one address at Ridgeways on Kiambu Road. Book by phone, drop the car in, or ask a question before you drive over.

Service list

Every job below is run from the workshop on Kiambu Road. Prices depend on the car and the parts, so the workshop quotes after it has seen the vehicle.

Tyre centre

Tyres bought and fitted at the shop, punctures repaired on the visit, and wheels aligned and balanced before the car leaves. The tyre bay is also where recovery gear such as winches comes through.

Sales and fitting Puncture repair Alignment and balancing

Servicing and oil changes

The routine work that keeps a car honest — oil changes, filters, and the minor and major services that stack up over a year. Drop the car in on a weekday and the bay works through it while you are at work.

Oil and filters Minor services Major services

Suspension and wheel work

Shocks, springs and bushes, plus the alignment faults that quietly eat a set of tyres. Diagnosed on the ramp rather than guessed at over the counter.

Shocks and springs Fault diagnosis Wheel work

Body repair and respray

Accident repair, panel work and full body spray. Rear-end collisions, scraped bumpers and tired paint are the everyday jobs here — the workshop films this work for its own channels as it goes.

Accident repair Panel work Full respray

Gearbox repair

A dedicated gearbox facility handles the jobs most garages send away: teardown, fault finding and rebuilds on manual and automatic units.

Fault finding Rebuilds Dedicated facility

Car lights

Lights are sold and repaired — bulbs, headlamps, tail lamps and the wiring behind them. Bring the unit in, or call ahead with the make and model and the counter will check what is on the shelf.

Sold and repaired All types of lighting

Car wash

Interior and exterior wash and detail, so the car leaves looking the way the mechanical work made it run.

Interior Exterior

Facelift kits and styling

Front-end conversions and styling upgrades, supplied as kits and fitted in the bay so the panel gaps are right when it is done.

Facelift kits Accessories fitted

Say what you can hear

You do not need to know the name of the part. Describe the knock, the pull, the warning light or the scrape and the workshop will tell you which bay it belongs in, what it will take, and roughly how long the car should be left.
