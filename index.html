What the workshop takes on

One address, both jobs. Book the work in, or call the counter while you are on the way — either way you reach the same team.

  • Tyre centre

    Sales, fitting and puncture repair, with wheel alignment and balancing done on site.

  • Servicing and oil changes

    Routine maintenance and minor or major services, oil changes included.

  • Suspension and wheel work

    Shocks, springs and alignment faults traced on the ramp and put right.

  • Body repair and respray

    Accident repair, panel work and full body spray, colour matched.

  • Gearbox repair

    A dedicated gearbox facility for rebuilds, faults and second-hand units.

  • Car lights

    We sell and repair all types of vehicle lighting, bulbs through to full units.

  • Car wash

    Interior and exterior wash and detail once the mechanical work is done.

  • Facelift kits and styling

    Front-end conversions, styling upgrades and accessories, fitted in the bay.
