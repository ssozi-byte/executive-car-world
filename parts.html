Getting the right part

Three things to have ready

Step 01

Make, model and year

The chassis plate or the windscreen sticker gives you most of it. That alone rules out half the wrong parts.

Step 02

The part, or a photo of it

Name it if you can. If you cannot, a photograph of the old part on the phone is enough for the counter to identify it.

Step 03

Whether it needs fitting

The garage is in the same building, so a part bought across the counter can go straight into a bay.

Ask for the part before you drive over.
